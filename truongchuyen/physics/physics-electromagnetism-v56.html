1. Nguồn thực và công suất tải

Lý thuyết và suy dẫn

Nguồn E nối tiếp điện trở trong r: I=E/(R+r), U=E−Ir. Công suất tải \(P=E^2R/(R+r)^2\). Đạo hàm theo R có dấu r−R, nên P cực đại tại R=r (r>0); hiệu suất khi đó 50%. Không đồng nhất tối đa công suất với tối đa hiệu suất.

PHY103-B · DC circuits

Dr. Dang Minh Tuan

V5.6 draft; independent review pending.

1. Real sources and load power

Theory and derivation

Source E in series with internal resistance r: I=E/(R+r), U=E−Ir. Load power \(P=E^2R/(R+r)^2\). Its derivative has sign r−R, so power peaks at R=r (r>0), with 50% efficiency. Maximum power is not maximum efficiency.

Worked example

E=12 V,r=2 Ω,R=4 Ω: I=2 A,U=8 V,P=16 W. R=2 Ω gives 18 W.

Independent practice

PHY103-B-V56-1.1. [D2] E=6 V,r=1 Ω,R=5 Ω. Find I,U.

PHY103-B-V56-1.2. [D4] E=9 V,r=3 Ω. Choose maximum-power load.

2. Meters and loading

Theory and derivation

An ammeter is in series, voltmeter in parallel. Finite Rv gives \(R_t=RR_v/(R+R_v)\). With internal source resistance this changes measured voltage. Redraw the circuit with instruments.

Worked example

10 V source,r=1 kΩ,9 kΩ load: U=9 V. A 9 kΩ meter gives Rt=4.5 kΩ,U≈8.18 V.

Independent practice

PHY103-B-V56-2.1. [D2] R=1 kΩ,Rv=9 kΩ. Find Rt.

Lý thuyết và suy dẫn

Điện trường điểm \(\mathbf E=kq\mathbf r/r^3\). Cộng vector. Lưỡng cực ±q cách d có moment p=qd hướng từ âm tới dương. Xa hệ r≫d, khai triển trường trên trục cho E≈2kp/r³; trên trung trực E≈kp/r³ ngược p. Không áp dụng gần điện tích.

1. Field superposition and dipoles

Theory and derivation

Point field \(\mathbf E=kq\mathbf r/r^3\). Add vectors. Dipole ±q separated by d has moment p=qd from negative to positive. Far away r≫d, expansion yields axial E≈2kp/r³ and equatorial E≈kp/r³ opposite p. Not valid near charges.

Worked example

±2 nC separated 2 cm: midpoint E=3.6×10⁵ N/C from + to −.

Independent practice

PHY204-A-V56-1.1. [D2] Two equal +q charges: midpoint E?

PHY204-A-V56-1.2. [D4] Far along dipole axis, double r; change in E?

2. Gauss's law and symmetry

Theory and derivation

\(\oint_S\mathbf E\cdot d\mathbf A=Q_{\rm enc}/\epsilon_0\). Uniform solid sphere radius R: enclosed charge 4πρr³/3 for r<R, so E=ρr/(3ε₀); outside E=kQ/r². Extract E from integral only with sufficient symmetry.

Worked example

Q=8 nC,R=0.2 m,r=0.1 m: Qenc=1 nC,E=900 N/C.

Independent practice

PHY204-A-V56-2.1. [D2] Does an external charge contribute nonzero net closed flux?

PHY204-A-V56-2.2. [D4] An insulating solid sphere of radius R has uniform nonzero volume charge density in vacuum. Compare field magnitudes E(R/2) and E(2R) with E(R).

3. Potential and gradient

Theory and derivation

Electrostatics: \(\Delta V=-\int\mathbf E\cdot d\mathbf l,\ \mathbf E=-\nabla V\). Point V=kq/r with V∞=0. Field work is −qΔV; vacuum field energy density ε₀E²/2. Distinguish V from qV.

Worked example

q=3 nC,r 0.1→0.3 m: V=270→90 V; +2 nC test charge receives 360 nJ.

Independent practice

PHY204-B · Networks and capacitors

Dr. Dang Minh Tuan

1. Capacitors and dielectrics

Theory and derivation

Ideal parallel plates C=εA/d,Q=CV,U=Q²/(2C)=CV²/2. Full dielectric increases C by κ. At fixed Q, V,U fall by κ; at fixed V,Q,U rise by κ. Include source work in energy balance; neglect fringing.

Worked example

C=2 μF,V=10 V: Q=20 μC,U=100 μJ. Isolate then κ=4: V=2.5 V,U=25 μJ.

Independent practice

PHY204-B-V56-1.1. [D2] Double C at fixed V: Q,U?

2. Bridge circuits and nodal equations

Theory and derivation

At a node with no injected current \(\sum_j(V_i-V_j)/R_{ij}=0\). Bridge a–c–b has R₁,R₂; a–d–b has R₃,R₄; R₅ joins c,d. If R₁/R₂=R₃/R₄, Vc=Vd, no R₅ current. Otherwise solve both node equations.

Worked example

R₁=2,R₂=4,R₃=3,R₄=6 Ω: Rab=3.6 Ω. Va=9,Vb=0: Vc=Vd=6 V.

Independent practice

PHY204-B-V56-2.1. [D2] R₁=R₂=R₃=10 Ω. Balanced R₄?

PHY204-B-V56-2.2. [D4] Bridge circuit: a–c–b has R₁,R₂; a–d–b has R₃,R₄; R₅ joins c,d. Va=10 V,Vb=0; R₁=R₂=R₃=R₅=1 Ω,R₄=2 Ω. Find Vc,Vd and signed Icd positive from c to d.

3. Infinite resistor ladder

Theory and derivation

Each cell has series R then shunt R in parallel with remainder. \(X=R+RX/(R+X)\), giving positive X=R(1+√5)/2. Short-terminated finite recurrence X₀=0, Xₙ₊₁=R+RXₙ/(R+Xₙ) verifies convergence.

Worked example

Independent practice

4. RC transients and energy

Theory and derivation

\(\ RC\dot V_C+V_C=E,\ V_C=E+(V_0-E)e^{-t/(RC)}\). From empty, source work CE², storage CE²/2, resistor heat CE²/2. Recompute source work for nonzero V0. Time constant τ=RC.
