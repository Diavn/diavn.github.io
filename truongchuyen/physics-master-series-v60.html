Chuẩn học liệu / Learning and teaching protocol

L1: identify the system, sketch, choose axes, derive by algebra, check units. L2: solve symbolically, add calculus only after prerequisites, check limits. L3: unfamiliar transfer, choose among competing models, verify physical constraints. For teachers: record misconception, hint ladder, alternative solution, scoring criteria, and source locator. Keep worked solutions separate from assessment items; reserve unseen contest sets.

Morin ch1 and preface: use symbolic reasoning, dimensions and special cases, reveal only a hint before retrying. FMA19 pp. xi–xii: use topic/difficulty tables and reserve unseen exams; do not use contest sets as the first physics course. KZ pp.1–7: prefer coherent foundations before competition problems. These are recommendations, not validated V-TALENT outcomes.
