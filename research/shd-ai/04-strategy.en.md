## 13. Three proposed flagship studies

Rather than pursuing all seven strands simultaneously, the programme should concentrate initially on three flagship studies.

<div class="flagships">
<article><small>FLAGSHIP 1 — COGNITIVE SCIENCE</small><h3>From AI-Generated Solutions to Generative Human Understanding</h3><p>Identify the mechanisms through which supplied knowledge becomes generative human understanding.</p><p class="caption">Prospective journals: Learning and Instruction; Journal of Educational Psychology.</p></article>
<article><small>FLAGSHIP 2 — EDUCATIONAL MEASUREMENT</small><h3>When AI-Supported Performance Becomes Misleading Evidence of Competence</h3><p>Identify the conditions under which artefacts lose diagnostic value and investigate methods for restoring evidential validity.</p><p class="caption">Prospective journals: Educational Assessment; Journal of Educational Measurement.</p></article>
<article><small>FLAGSHIP 3 — EDUCATION SYSTEMS</small><h3>Generative AI-Induced Pedagogical Convergence and Learning Opportunity Inequality</h3><p>Examine how AI-induced pedagogical convergence affects the distribution of opportunities for intellectual development across education systems.</p><p class="caption">Prospective journals: British Journal of Educational Technology; Sociology of Education.</p></article>

The three flagships constitute interconnected levels of analysis:

| Level | Primary unit | Scientific question |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | Learner | What do people learn from AI? |
| Meso | Teacher, classroom and assessment system | How can competence development be identified and fostered? |
| Macro | School and education system | How are developmental opportunities distributed as AI becomes widespread? |

PGJ provides a bridge between individual, teacher and classroom levels.

## 14. Existing research resources in Vietnam

The programme need not start from scratch. Research on PGJ, LEAN-PGJ, Scratch, competence assessment, PISA/TALIS and intelligent tutoring can offer complementary research settings.

| Resource | Potential research use |
|---|---|
| PGJ and LEAN-PGJ | Professional judgment, measurement validity and construct validation |
| TMT2097 Scratch course data | Changes in product quality and evidence of competence |
| Pre-service primary teachers | Experiments on judgment and internalisation of pedagogical knowledge |
| Advanced mathematics and Olympiad students | Generative capability, talent identification and far transfer |
| Lower-secondary mathematics AI tutor project | Adaptive scaffolding and preservation of cognitive activity |
| PISA/TALIS | Educational context, learning opportunities and socioeconomic differences |
| VERS community of educators and researchers | Multisite research and replication |

The status, quality, access rights and completeness of each dataset must be assessed before integration. Distinct research samples should not be treated as interchangeable, and the same data should not be reused to generate substantially overlapping publications.

In policy terms, the programme can be aligned with the Vietnamese Digital Competence Framework for Learners under Circular 02/2025/TT-BGDĐT, which comprises six domains, 24 component competencies, four proficiency levels and eight sublevels.

The [*OECD Digital Education Outlook 2026*](https://doi.org/10.1787/062a7394-en) likewise emphasises learner competence, teacher agency, human oversight and judgment in AI systems.

## 17. Conclusion and strategic recommendations

The distinctive contribution of the educational perspective expressed in the interview is its connection between the nature of learning, assessment validity, teacher competence and the purpose of human development.

Six shifts summarise the proposed research orientation:

| Conventional emphasis | Research emphasis in the AI era |
|---|---|
| Knowledge transmission | Internalisation and generation of knowledge |
| Task completion | Durable competence development |
| Product assessment | Validation of evidential inferences |
| Proficient technology use | Judgment and orchestration of technology |
| Selection based on achievement | Identification of developmental potential |
| Equal technological access | Equity in opportunities for intellectual development |

### Implementation recommendations

| Research direction | Proposed decision |
|---|---|
| Existing PGJ research | **CONTINUE** — prioritise empirical validation |
| Assessment diagnosticity | **CONTINUE** — near-term flagship |
| Generative understanding | **CONTINUE** — emerging theoretical direction |
| Creativity and talent identification | **CONTINUE** — develop a pilot study |
| Pedagogical convergence and inequality | **CONTINUE** — undertake a dedicated in-depth review |
| Humanistic values and relational pedagogy | **PAUSE** — retain in the long-term programme |
| Agentic AI | **MERGE** — integrate with existing educational technology projects |

**Limitations of the present review:** substantial excerpts of the original interview and its republications were retrieved, but direct access to the complete VNU webpage was unsuccessful. The international review is a theoretically oriented critical integrative review, not a completed PRISMA systematic review. Full texts of all studies, journal rankings, bibliographic details of recent candidate references and forward/backward citation searches have not been comprehensively verified. The research gaps therefore remain candidates requiring further investigation; no breakthrough is claimed. The SHD-AI title, research strands, priority ratings and illustrative models are proposals, not approved decisions or empirical results. The Decision Registry has not been updated.

## 18. Proposed next research stage

The next stage should develop a comprehensive research dossier:

**Master Research Blueprint: The Science of Human Development in the AI Era 2026–2035 — Version 1.0**

The dossier should comprise:

- A corpus of 100–150 foundational and recent works, prioritising leading interdisciplinary journals.
- A matrix of theories, empirical findings, research methods and limitations.
- A novelty and rival-theory comparison matrix.
- A system of falsifiable hypotheses.
- Three complete flagship research protocols.
- Research tasks, measurement instruments and data collection procedures suited to Vietnam.
- A multisite validation plan and international publication roadmap.

One question can position the entire programme over the coming decade:

> **How can education transform the growing intelligence of machines into the enduring development of human intelligence, agency, and potential?**

This question encompasses PGJ without being limited to it, connects educational research with cognitive science, measurement, talent development and educational policy, and retains the humanistic purpose of the original interview.
