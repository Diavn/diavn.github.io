Introducing AI into schools: Learning to use and verify it

VOV1 · Guest: Dr Tuan Minh Dang · VOV1 · Voice of Vietnam · Dòng chảy sự kiện

AI in educationAssessmentTeacher educationEducation policy

Overview

VOV1's current-affairs programme addresses the tension between the convenience of AI-generated explanations and students' ability to recognise errors. Its introduction identifies independent learning and thinking as central considerations in school AI integration. Dr Tuan Minh Dang of VNU University of Education is the featured guest.

Key insights and scope

The programme description raises the question of how learners can judge the reliability of AI outputs before they possess sufficient subject knowledge. This is a summary of the indexed official introduction, not a transcript or a claim about particular recommendations made during the broadcast. The recording has not been reviewed.
