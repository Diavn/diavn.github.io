Rethinking digital competence for pre-service teachers in the GenAI era: A conceptual framework for pedagogical GenAI judgment

Journal of Digital Learning in Teacher Education, 42(3), 168–181

The conceptual article proposes DigiGen-PST 2030, placing pedagogical GenAI judgment within a wider framework of digital competence, pedagogical transformation and future-oriented skills. Designing GenAI-supported learning activities in teacher education: Cognitive effort, pedagogical judgment and academic integrity

Tạp chí Giáo dục, 26 (special issue 8), 276–283

A design-oriented theoretical review integrates selected research in cognition, learning sciences and teacher education with Vietnamese policy documents. It proposes seven points of pedagogical control and argues that using GenAI effectively depends on how tasks, assistance and evidence of reasoning are organised.

Fostering STEM Education Competency for Elementary Education Students at Universities of Pedagogy in Vietnam

ICMMCS 2023 · Springer, pp. 107–124

Using questionnaires and interviews with final-year primary-education students in Vietnam, the study reports moderate overall STEM teaching competence, with some components below that level. It identifies training content and instructional organisation as concerns and proposes improvements to technology-supported preparation.
