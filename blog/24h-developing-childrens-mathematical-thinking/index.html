Media & interviews · 17 September 2017

Developing children's mathematical thinking: Interest, number sense and independent reasoning

Hồng Nhung (Khám Phá) · Interview with Đặng Minh Tuấn · 24h · Giáo dục – Du học

Mathematics educationGifted education & talent developmentSchool governance & family engagementAssessment

Overview

The interview presents Đặng Minh Tuấn's advice to parents on cultivating interest in mathematics through everyday quantities, exploration and tasks adjusted to individual readiness. It emphasises children's willingness to express their thinking and solve problems independently rather than accumulating content ahead of school.

Key insights and scope

The discussion recommends identifying learning potential, avoiding comparisons between children, and supporting a problem-solving process from reading and interpreting the question to checking the result. These are practice-based educational perspectives rather than experimentally established effects of a particular method.
