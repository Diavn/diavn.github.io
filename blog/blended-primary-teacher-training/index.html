Research insights · 29 August 2023

Training Elementary Teachers in Vietnam by Blended Learning Model

Tiep Quang Pham, Tuan Minh Dang, Hung Van Nguyen & Huong Thi Nguyen · ICMMCS 2023 · Springer, pp. 219–234

Teacher educationEducational technology

Overview

The study examines how lecturers in Vietnamese primary teacher-education programmes view the balance between face-to-face and online instruction. Questionnaire and interview responses reveal differing preferences and emphasise technological conditions and participants’ digital skills.

Key insights and scope

The authors propose a balance of delivery modes and recommendations for improving blended teacher education. The numerical ratio is not reproduced because it is not available in the abstract; the full chapter may require a subscription.
